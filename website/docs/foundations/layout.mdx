# Layout, spacing and density

- Page wrap: max 1440px (`content-max`), 12px padding and 12px gaps (`space-3`). Cards pad 16px (`space-4`), modals 20px (`space-5`).
- Grids: `.grid2` columns of at least 260px, `.grid3` at least 220px, form grids at least 240px; section menus 220 to 260px beside a flexible main column.
- Clinical Sidebar offsets content by `side-w` 240px (64px collapsed); Focus Rail by 64px; Classic and Command Bar by none. Under 900px the sidebar and rail fold into a menu button.
- Density: Comfortable controls are `control-h` 36px with 9px table cell padding; Compact is 30px (`control-h-compact`) with 4px cells and 10px card padding. Phones use `control-h-touch` 44px minimum; the kiosk `control-h-kiosk` 48px. Classic does not apply Compact.
- Radius: 4px inputs and tags, 6px buttons and menus, 8px cards, 10px shell menus, 12px modals, 999px pills.
- Shadow: `shadow-card` on cards (none in Command Bar and Dark, which use a border), `shadow-pop` on menus, modals and drawers.
- Z-index: page menus 30, nav and overlays 40, toasts 60, tooltips 70, command palette 80.
- Motion (notes, not tokens): sidebar width 150ms, switch knob 150ms, spinner 0.8s per turn, toast stays 2800ms, skeleton pulse 1.4s. All slow down or stop under prefers-reduced-motion.
