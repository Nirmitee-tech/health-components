# Role-based UI: hide, lock, deny

Every screen has an access level per role: **none**, **view**, **edit** (and **approve** in the permission matrix).

- **None inside a screen: hide.** Do not render the menu item, button or section. Do not show it disabled.
- **View: lock.** Show the `PermissionState kind="lock"` banner at the top, render fields as `LockedField` or `readOnly`, hide save and edit buttons. Values stay readable and copyable.
- **None for a whole screen reached by link: deny.** Show `PermissionState kind="denied"` with "Go to my home screen" and "Compare roles".
- **Staff looking at patient screens: preview.** `kind="preview"`; patient actions (book, pay, send, sign) are blocked.
- **Restricted charts: break the glass.** `BreakTheGlassDialog` asks for a reason; access is written to the audit log (`AuditLogRow` action btg).
- The "Viewing as" `RoleSwitcher` previews another role for the session only and never grants rights.
